Since November 1, 2025, New Mexico's Child Care Assistance program pays the full cost of care at participating licensed providers for families where every available parent works, is looking for work, or attends school, regardless of income. Piñon Grove participates. Eligibility is decided by the state Early Childhood Education and Care Department (ECECD), not by the center, and Marcus can help you apply.

Copays for Child Care Assistance families are currently $0. A state law signed in March 2026 allows copays to return under certain economic conditions. If that happens, we will tell families in writing before anything changes.

We do not charge Child Care Assistance families any add-on fees, including registration, supply, or activity fees. Families paying tuition directly pay a $75 registration fee once per year and pay tuition by the 1st of each month through autopay in the app.

The Sunflowers Pre-K school day from 8:00 am to 2:30 pm is free for all 4-year-olds through New Mexico Pre-K.

## Enrollment, waitlist and tours {#enrollment}
<!-- updated: 2026-08-03 by Marcus Benally -->
To join the waitlist, fill out the interest form in the app or call Marcus. There is no waitlist fee. Current wait times are about 3 to 4 months for the Hummingbirds infant room and 1 to 2 months for other rooms.

Before a child starts, families complete the enrollment packet, provide an immunization record or exemption, list emergency contacts and authorized pickups, and attend a short orientation with the lead teacher.

We offer tours on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 9:30 am and 4:00 pm. Tours take about 30 minutes, and children are welcome to come along. Book a tour in the app or by phone.

## Arrival, departure and pickup authorization {#pickup}
<!-- updated: 2026-08-03 by Elena Vigil -->
An adult must walk each child into and out of the classroom and check them in and out in the app. We release children only to adults 18 or older who are on the child's authorized pickup list, and staff will ask for photo ID until they know the person.

The enrolling parent manages the pickup list in the app. A same-day change for someone who is not already on the list must be confirmed by phone with Elena or Marcus before pickup. Teachers and the app's assistant cannot approve a new pickup person.

## Late pickup {#late-pickup}
<!-- updated: 2026-08-03 by Elena Vigil -->
We close at 6:00 pm. If you are running late, call the front desk so your child's teacher can reassure them. We do not charge late fees. After three late pickups in a semester, Elena will meet with you to make a plan.

If a child is still here 30 minutes after closing and we cannot reach a parent or any emergency contact, state licensing rules require us to contact the Albuquerque Police Department.

## Attendance and absences {#attendance}
<!-- updated: 2026-08-03 by Elena Vigil -->
Please report absences in the app by 9:00 am, and tell us the reason if your child is sick so we can watch for illness in the classroom. Let us know ahead of time about vacations or longer absences.

If a child is absent for two weeks in a row with no contact from the family, we may end the enrollment.

During Balloon Fiesta week, arriving by 9:30 am is fine for families attending early-morning launches.

## Illness and when to stay home {#illness}
<!-- updated: 2026-09-02 by Elena Vigil -->
Children who are sick need to stay home until they can take part in the whole day, including outdoor play. Keep your child home, or expect a call to pick up, for any of the following:

- A fever of 100.4°F or higher, now or in the past 24 hours. Your child may return after 24 hours without a fever and without fever-reducing medicine such as acetaminophen or ibuprofen.
- Vomiting or diarrhea in the past 24 hours.
- Pink eye or eye discharge, until a doctor says it is not contagious or treatment has started.
- An infection treated with antibiotics, until your child has had at least 24 hours of antibiotics.
- A rash that has not been diagnosed by a doctor.
- A cough that keeps your child from resting at nap time, or cloudy nasal discharge together with a fever.
- Any contagious condition such as chicken pox, strep throat, hand-foot-and-mouth, or lice, until a doctor clears your child.

If your child becomes sick at school, we will call you and your child will rest away from the group until you arrive. Please pick up within one hour. A doctor's note is required after an emergency room visit or hospital stay.

We must report some contagious diseases to the New Mexico Department of Health, and we notify families when there is a contagious illness in a classroom without sharing who is sick.

If your child has trouble breathing, a seizure, a severe allergic reaction, or any other emergency, call 911 first.

## Medication {#medication}
<!-- updated: 2026-08-03 by Elena Vigil -->
Teachers can give medication only with a signed medication form in the app. Medication must be in its original container with the pharmacy label and your child's name. Hand it to a teacher; never leave it in a backpack or cubby.

This applies to over-the-counter products too, including sunscreen, diaper cream, and pain relievers. We do not give cough drops because of the choking risk. Children with an epinephrine auto-injector or inhaler need a current action plan signed by their doctor.

## Meals and nutrition {#meals}
<!-- updated: 2026-08-03 by Elena Vigil -->
Piñon Grove provides breakfast, lunch, and an afternoon snack every day through the federal Child and Adult Care Food Program, at no cost to families. You do not need to pack lunch. Weekly menus are posted in the app and on the bulletin board.

- Breakfast is served from 8:00 to 8:45 am. Children arriving after 8:45 should eat breakfast at home.
- Lunch is at 11:30 am, served family style.
- Afternoon snack is at 3:00 pm.

We provide iron-fortified infant formula and baby food for Hummingbirds infants. Families who prefer breast milk can bring it labeled with the child's name and date.

Outside food is not allowed, except store-bought celebration treats arranged with the teacher in advance.

## Food allergies and special diets {#allergies}
<!-- updated: 2026-08-03 by Elena Vigil -->
Our kitchen does not serve peanuts or tree nuts. Children with food allergies need an allergy action plan signed by their doctor, and our meal vendor prepares substitutions for each child's needs. Allergy plans and photos are posted in each classroom and kitchen so every adult knows.

We also accommodate religious and cultural diets when families let us know.

## Outdoor play, sun and weather {#weather}
<!-- updated: 2026-08-03 by Elena Vigil -->
Children play outside every morning and afternoon. Albuquerque's sun is strong at our altitude, so please send a labeled hat, and sign the sunscreen permission so teachers can apply your labeled sunscreen before outdoor time.

We stay indoors during lightning, high winds, blowing dust, or temperatures above 95°F or below 25°F.

**Snow and ice.** We follow Albuquerque Public Schools (APS) weather decisions:
- If APS closes for weather, we close.
- If APS announces a two-hour delay, we open at 9:00 am and do not serve breakfast.
- If APS releases early because of weather, we close early and call families to pick up.
- If APS is on break, Elena decides and posts in the app by 6:00 am.

## Clothing and items from home {#clothing}
<!-- updated: 2026-08-03 by Elena Vigil -->
Please dress children for messy play and in layers for changing weather. Closed-toe shoes are required for safety. Keep a full labeled change of clothes in your child's cubby, and replace it when it comes home.

Families provide diapers and wipes. Children may bring a small blanket and a soft toy for nap time, labeled with their name. Please keep other toys at home, along with gum, candy, jewelry, and toy weapons.

## Toilet learning {#toileting}
<!-- updated: 2026-08-03 by Elena Vigil -->
We start toilet learning when a child shows signs of readiness and the family is ready too. The lead teacher will meet with you to agree on one plan for home and school. Please send pull-ups, wipes, and at least two extra sets of clothes while your child is learning.

## Celebrations and culture {#celebrations}
<!-- updated: 2026-08-03 by Elena Vigil -->
We celebrate the seasons and the traditions of our families and our city, including Balloon Fiesta in October, Indigenous Peoples' Day, and Día de los Muertos, when families are invited to add photos to our lobby ofrenda. Families are always welcome to share their own traditions.

For birthdays, you may bring a store-bought, nut-free treat with a nutrition label. Please arrange it with your child's teacher at least two days ahead. Party invitations can be handed out at the center only if the whole class is invited.

## Guidance and behavior {#behavior}
<!-- updated: 2026-08-03 by Elena Vigil -->
Teachers use positive guidance: clear, simple limits, redirection, and noticing what children do well. We never use physical punishment, shaming, or withholding food, rest, or bathroom use. If a pattern of behavior worries us, the lead teacher and Elena will meet with you to make a plan together.

## Communication and partnership {#communication}
<!-- updated: 2026-08-03 by Elena Vigil -->
The brightwheel app is our main way to communicate. Teachers post photos and daily notes, and you can message them any time. Teachers reply during breaks, since they are with the children most of the day.

We hold parent-teacher conferences twice a year, in October and April. Families are welcome to visit the classroom at any time.

## Custody and legal documents {#custody}
<!-- updated: 2026-08-03 by Elena Vigil -->
We follow only the custody and parenting documents that are on file with us. If a court order limits who can pick up or contact your child, please give Elena a copy as soon as possible. Without an order on file, we cannot keep a legal parent from picking up their child. Staff stay neutral and will not take sides in family disputes.

## Safety, emergencies and reporting {#safety}
<!-- updated: 2026-08-03 by Elena Vigil -->
All staff are trained in first aid and CPR. If a child is hurt, teachers give first aid, call 911 when needed, and contact you right away. We complete an incident report for any injury and share it with you the same day.

We practice fire, shelter-in-place, and lockdown drills every month. If we must leave the building, we walk to our evacuation site at the nearby community center and call every family.

Staff are required by New Mexico law to report any suspected child abuse or neglect to the Children, Youth and Families Department.

## Concerns and feedback {#concerns}
<!-- updated: 2026-08-03 by Elena Vigil -->
Please start with your child's lead teacher. If something isn't resolved, contact Elena directly. You can also contact the New Mexico Early Childhood Education and Care Department's licensing office about any concern.

We keep information about every child and family private. Please don't ask staff about other children or families, and don't share photos of other children taken at the center.
